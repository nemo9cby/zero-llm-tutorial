--- サンプル 1 ---
One day, a little girl named Lily went to her room. She saw a muffin on the table. Lily picked up the muffin and said, "Yum! I will eat it later."
Later, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw Lily with the muffin and said, "Lily, did you eat the muffin too fast?" Lily felt sorry and said, "Yes, Mom." But her mom was not home. Her mom told her, "I learn to be careful with things you find. Now, you can have some muffin tonight."
Lily was sad but she wanted to try again. Next time, she looked around the room and saw the muffin on a high shelf. She climbed on a chair and tried to get it. She got closer to the muffin. When she reached the top, she saw her mom waiting for her. She smiled and said, "Mom! I got the muffin!" Her mom hugged her and said, "Lily, you are learning new things. Always remember to be careful and to listen to Mom."
The moral of the story is to always listen to the grown-ups and to always be careful of things that you can't do.
--- サンプル 2 ---
Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play and run in his yard. One day, he found a plant in his yard. The plant was small and green. Tim was very happy and wanted to show his mom the plant.
Tim's mom was not happy, but she had an idea. She gave Tim a spicy pepper to taste instead of a real one. Tim did not know that the pepper was magic. He liked to taste things.
One day, Tim and his mom went to the yard. The plant had a lot of pepper plants. But, something unexpected happened! Tim picked a big red pepper and wanted to see how tasty it would taste. His mom smiled and said, "I forgot, it is not just a pepper. But it is very spicy and yummy!" They put the spicy pepper pepper in a pot and ate it up.
--- サンプル 3 ---
One day, a boy named Tim found a kite in his room. It was a very interesting kite with many colors. Tim wanted to fly the kite in the park. His mom said, "Tim, let me lead the kite to the park."
Tim held the kite up high and started to run. He was happy and excited. The kite danced in the wind, and Tim laughed. The kite seemed to fly higher and higher.
But then, something unexpected happened. The wind stopped, and the kite started to disappear! Tim was sad. He looked at his mom and said, "Mom, I am sorry. The kite disappeared." His mom smiled and said, "It's okay, accidents happen. Next time, I will always tell you a secret." Tim smiled and hugged his mom. They went to the park, and Tim had a fun day.
